\expandafter\def\csname figcaption:fig-ops\endcsname{Time and operations per base for held-out genomes: ms2-lcp, ms2-psi, ms2-dual and ms2-phi (MS only, ordinary queries). Left pair: nanoseconds per query base, one bar per layout; a black tick across the bar marks the same layout's oracle replay. Right pair: operations per query base, stacked as LF steps (light segment) and the steps the layout adds on top (full color): psi steps for ms2-psi, phi steps for ms2-phi, both for ms2-dual, and walk rows (LCP rows scanned while repositioning) for ms2-lcp. The oracle takes exactly one LF step per base and nothing else, so the light segment is also the oracle's operation count; the full-color segment is the work the oracle avoids. Queries are genomes absent from the index. mtb152 is the TeraTools paper's 95 percent split queried with the 8 held-out genomes, each followed by its reverse complement (600 records, 59.1 Mbases). HPRC2 full is Human472 queried with APR008 haplotype 1 from the Arab Pangenome Reference (GenBank GCA\_050494745.1, OpenHGL 210019\_APR008.hap1), absent from Human472 (600 ACGT-only records, 0.06 Gbases). Records are split at every run of characters other than A, C, G and T. Repositions (mismatches that force the query to restart its match) are rare: 0.00065 per base on mtb152 and 0.00055 on HPRC2 full, which is why replay saves so little for ms2-lcp, whose only extra work is the walk at a reposition. References: mtb152 and HPRC2 full (Human472, 472 haplotypes). Single thread, interleave 32 (queries in flight at once), NUMA-bound (numactl CPU and memory binding to one node). Time is query time only; index load time is excluded and reported separately. Each time is the median of two timed runs after one warmup run. Operation counts come from a counting build of the same commit; Orbit commit 2885ee8.}
